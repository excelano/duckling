# Reach 4 species list

Alder Creek Field Station. Recorded on the March survey, not a complete list.

## Bankside

- Alder, *Alnus glutinosa*, dominant along the whole reach
- Willow, *Salix* spp., pollarded at the bend
- Meadowsweet, in the wet margin above the culvert

## In the water

| Group | Sites | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mayfly nymph | AC-01, AC-03, AC-04 | absent below the culvert |
| Caddis larva | all five | cased, mostly on the riffles |
| Freshwater shrimp | AC-02, AC-05 | abundant |

The absence of mayfly nymph at AC-02 and AC-05 is noted rather than
explained. Two surveys is not a trend.
